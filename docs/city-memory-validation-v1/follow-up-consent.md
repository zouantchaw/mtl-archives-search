# Follow-up, consent, and privacy notes

Before notes or recording, ask permission in plain language: what is being tested, whether notes are taken, whether audio/video is recorded, where it will be stored, who can access it, and how to request deletion. A refusal to record does not prevent participation; use contemporaneous notes. Never put raw audio/video, names, email addresses, phone numbers, client secrets, or sensitive budgets in the ledger, repository, or GitHub issue.

Use a pseudonymous reference (`stakeholder-1`) and only role category, organization type, decision access, categorical codes, and private reference tokens in the ledger. Store any recording or detailed transcript in the approved private location; put only its private reference token in the ledger. Confirm whether a follow-up proposal may be sent and whether the stakeholder may be named in any future case study; default to no public attribution.

Neutral follow-up template:

> Thank you for reviewing City Memory Study 001. As discussed, the proposed next step is the fixed three-week diagnostic: 12 source-linked Montreal archive records, one visual direction, provenance ledger, concept deck, one spatial application, and one revision round for $3,500 CAD, paid 50% on approval and 50% on delivery. Printing, fabrication, installation, taxes, third-party licensing, full interior-design documents, custom software, and unverified research/rights clearance are excluded. Please reply only if you want the one-page scope routed through your normal approval/procurement process. No commitment is assumed by this note.

Log the date sent, permission to follow up, response class, and next action. If no response arrives, log `no_response` in private notes but do not convert it into a decline or acceptance without an explicit signal.
