MTL Archives · City Memory Study 001

Five ways the city
could live in a room.

Preliminary composition sketches for a Montréal hospitality or workplace programme. They use reviewed source derivatives to test sequence, scale, and placement—not installed-work photography, scale-accurate room renders, or final fabrication files.

Preliminary concept boundary. These are composition sketches, not room photography or scale-accurate renders. Before fabrication, confirm the full-resolution original, crop, colour/tonal treatment, exact attribution, and any additional rights or people/mark review.
01 · Lobby statement piece

The threshold, enlarged

Use the broad facade as a calm arrival anchor. Let the image carry the wall; keep interpretation in a small adjacent evidence label.

Candidate: record 31 · wide ground view
Format hypothesis: 36–48 in long edge, archival print or framed panel.

02 · Corridor sequence

Moving through

Three ground views create a walking rhythm: intersection, vertical corridor, civic frontage. The sequence is editorial, not a claim that the images form one documented route.

Candidate: records 10 / 17 / 45
Format hypothesis: three 18–24 in panels with generous breathing room.

03 · Guest-room grouping

Commerce, close up

A tall storefront detail paired with a wide frontage lets a guest move from sign and texture to street scale.

Candidate: records 58 / 105
Format hypothesis: portrait + landscape pair; caption card carries the source boundary.

04 · Workplace / common area

Pattern at distance

An aerial view provides visual quiet and a shift in scale for a shared space. Do not overlay an unverified map or place name.

Candidate: record 4501
Format hypothesis: large matte panel or framed photographic print.

05 · Interpretive / digital layer

Evidence beside the image

A digital layer can reveal the source URL, review status, archive-reported metadata, and uncertainty without turning the experience into a technology demo.

Candidate: record 13272 with related 12115
Format hypothesis: responsive evidence card, QR or in-room screen.
