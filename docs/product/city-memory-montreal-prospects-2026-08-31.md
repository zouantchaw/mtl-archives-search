# City Memory Montréal prospect shortlist — 31 August 2026

This is an internal public-source research snapshot. It contains no private
contact data and authorizes no outreach. Before contact, the owner must approve
the exact prospect, stakeholder, package, message, sender, channel, and
follow-up boundary.

## Commercial routes

This shortlist serves two routes that share the same Provenance method:

- **Place route:** art, experience, architecture, hospitality, development,
  and public-realm organizations buy or commission a City Memory Concept Study
  for one real environment.
- **Institution route:** archives, museums, libraries, and cultural
  organizations buy a Collection Fit Review or Archive Activation Pilot for a
  collection they control.

Warm relationships and approved introductions take precedence over list order.
The visual subject of a pilot is not automatically its first outreach
recipient.

## Primary visual pilot subject

### Hôtel Nelligan / Corner Collection

- Public context: [Hôtel Nelligan — a new chapter](https://hotelnelligan.com/nelligan-reopening/)
- Buyer hypothesis: hotel or portfolio leadership, brand/guest experience,
  cultural/event programming, or property marketing. This is unconfirmed and
  does not make the organization the presumed first recipient.
- Why now: the public site describes a 2026 reimagining of the historic Old
  Montréal property, including redesigned rooms, event spaces, dining and
  cultural programming, and a spa planned for 2027.
- Tailoring angle: a source-linked Old Montréal memory layer spanning
  arrival/lobby, a guest-facing sequence, and concierge or cultural
  programming.
- Risk: active relaunch may limit attention; the work must support the current
  brand rather than become a generic nostalgia treatment. Public marketing
  images cannot be reused in a sendable mockup without approval.
- Route: build the hotel-specific concept as a strong demonstration. It may be
  introduced through an approved art, experience, architecture, or hospitality
  relationship before any direct approach to the property.

## Potential recipients and route partners

### GSM Studios

- Public context: [MEM project](https://www.gsmstudios.world/work/project/mem-centre-des-memoires-montrealaises) · [studio contact](https://www.gsmstudios.world/contact)
- Buyer hypothesis: Montréal studio lead, content/experience strategy,
  creative producer, or project director.
- Tailoring angle: a provenance layer for exhibition production—source-bound
  image cards, rights/attribution state, historical-confidence flags, and a
  client approval trail.
- Strategic value: possible repeat/channel partner across museum, heritage,
  and public-space projects.
- Risk: strong internal capability; Provenance must fit their workflow rather
  than arrive as another generic archive tool.

### Provencher_Roy

- Public context: [landscape architecture](https://provencherroy.ca/en/landscape-architecture) · [Sainte-Catherine Street West and Phillips Square](https://provencherroy.ca/en/projects/rue-sainte-catherine-ouest-et-square-phillips) · [business contact route](https://provencherroy.ca/contact)
- Buyer hypothesis: landscape/urban-design principal, business-development
  lead, or public-realm project lead.
- Tailoring angle: a site-memory evidence pack with before/after imagery,
  source trails, claim-confidence labels, and public-consultation prompts.
- Risk: budgets and interpretation authority are commission-specific; the
  pilot needs a live site or decision rather than a retrospective case study.

### MEM — Centre des mémoires montréalaises

- Public context: [mandate and public team](https://memmtl.ca/apropos)
- Buyer hypothesis: Collections/Exhibitions/Programs, Visitor Experience, or
  Community Projects leadership.
- Tailoring angle: a neighbourhood memory atlas combining source-linked
  archival images with community testimony only where consent and attribution
  are explicit.
- Strategic value: strongest direct fit with Montréal memory, recurring
  content, and collection activation.
- Risk: municipal procurement and existing infrastructure; community,
  Indigenous, and historically marginalized narratives require institutional
  and community review.

### Gray Collection / Hotel William Gray

- Public context: [Gray Collection](https://hotelwilliamgray.com/graycollection/)
- Buyer hypothesis: group brand/marketing, guest experience, programming,
  events, or property leadership.
- Tailoring angle: a rotating Old Montréal then/now lobby, room, event, or
  neighbourhood-walk layer with source-linked images.
- Risk: guest-flow and hospitality ROI must be explicit; licensing and content
  maintenance need to remain simple.

## Recommended sequence

1. Build the internally approved Hôtel Nelligan pilot without contacting the
   organization, assuming recipient status, or copying unapproved property
   assets.
2. Identify the strongest owner-approved warm relationship in the art,
   experience, architecture, hospitality, or institutional network. If an
   existing relationship can sponsor one real site or programme, test that
   route before cold outreach.
3. Test the concept as a repeatable production service with GSM Studios and
   Provencher_Roy, treating them as potential channel/delivery partners rather
   than fallback prospects.
4. Approach MEM through the institution path once the package is
   bilingual, rights-reviewed, and strong enough for public-sector scrutiny.
5. Contact Hôtel Nelligan, Gray Collection, or development prospects only after
   the visual prototype is client-sendable and the exact recipient, message,
   sender, channel, and follow-up boundary are approved.
