# Provenance Activation v1

## Product model

Provenance is a service-led product for activating visual, archival, cultural,
and historically significant collections. Dataset Factory is its internal
engine, MTL Archives is the first reference implementation, and City Memory is
the first commercial application.

```text
Provenance — product, method, and delivery boundary
  ├─ Dataset Factory — ingest, canonicalize, enrich, verify
  ├─ Trust layer — sources, rights, uncertainty, transforms, review
  ├─ Activation layer — search, stories, concepts, APIs, experiences
  └─ Applications — City Memory, MTL Archives, future client collections
```

V1 is deliberately narrower than “any dataset.” It begins where collection
value depends on images, culture, history, place, source authority, or rights
context.

## Activation stages

1. **Ingest** records, media, metadata, sources, rights, and constraints.
2. **Canonicalize** stable identities, variants, duplicates, families, and
   unresolved discrepancies.
3. **Enrich** visible evidence, text, entities, themes, locations, and useful
   relationships.
4. **Verify** the boundary between supplied metadata, visible evidence,
   hypotheses, external sources, human review, and unknowns.
5. **Govern** rights, attribution, uncertainty, transformations, approvals,
   and release status.
6. **Activate** the collection through search, stories, client concepts,
   editorial packages, APIs, or interactive experiences.
7. **Learn** from use and buyer evidence without treating engagement as
   historical truth.

## Initial offer

The first delivery model is a bounded Provenance Activation engagement backed
by reusable internal software. Depending on the collection, it may include:

- collection and metadata assessment;
- canonical record/family map;
- source, rights, claim, uncertainty, and transformation model;
- verified enrichment sample;
- one or more activation concepts;
- portable Provenance Package;
- implementation, licensing, and production roadmap.

Repeated service delivery should determine whether any component deserves to
become hosted software or an API. An agent platform is not a V1 dependency.

## Active roadmap

The active roadmap contains only:

- Provenance Activation product definition;
- Dataset Factory Core;
- Provenance Package;
- one prospect-specific City Memory Client Pilot;
- buyer validation after the pilot passes internal release review.

Benchmarking, model promotion, Gate H execution, advanced feedback learning,
GPU work, and workflow automation are deferred research or operations. Their
historical artifacts remain useful but do not block the first client pilot.

## Product principles

- Lead with outcomes; keep technical machinery backstage.
- Preserve evidence, metadata, hypothesis, human review, and unknowns as
  distinct states.
- Make rights and attribution useful without claiming blanket legal clearance.
- Prefer bounded service delivery before platform development.
- Keep research benchmarks separate from client-release gates.
- Keep MTL Archives as the proof, not the only collection Provenance can serve.
- Reconsider workflow automation only after repeated delivery exposes a
  measured bottleneck.
