# Provenance Activation v1

## Product model

Provenance is a service-led product for activating visual, archival, cultural,
and historically significant collections. Dataset Factory is its internal
engine, MTL Archives is the first reference implementation, and City Memory is
the first commercial application.

```text
Provenance — product, method, and delivery boundary
  ├─ Dataset Factory — ingest, canonicalize, enrich, verify
  ├─ Trust layer — sources, rights, uncertainty, transforms, review
  ├─ Activation layer — search, stories, concepts, APIs, experiences
  ├─ Institution path — fit review, activation pilot, portable handoff
  └─ Place path — City Memory concept study for built environments
```

V1 is deliberately narrower than “any dataset.” It begins where collection
value depends on images, culture, history, place, source authority, or rights
context.

## Activation stages

1. **Ingest** records, media, metadata, sources, rights, and constraints.
2. **Canonicalize** stable identities, variants, duplicates, families, and
   unresolved discrepancies.
3. **Enrich** visible evidence, text, entities, themes, locations, and useful
   relationships.
4. **Verify** the boundary between supplied metadata, visible evidence,
   hypotheses, external sources, human review, and unknowns.
5. **Govern** rights, attribution, uncertainty, transformations, approvals,
   and release status.
6. **Activate** the collection through search, stories, client concepts,
   editorial packages, APIs, or interactive experiences.
7. **Learn** from use and buyer evidence without treating engagement as
   historical truth.

## Commercial paths

Provenance has two commercial paths. They use the same Dataset Factory and
Provenance Package; only the buyer language, projection, and deliverables
change.

### Path A — institutions

Museums, archives, libraries, universities, and cultural organizations buy a
bounded review or activation of a collection they control. The relationship
should begin with a warm path or approved introduction, not a cold platform
demo or premature procurement pitch.

### Path B — art, built environment, and hospitality

Art advisors, experience and architecture studios, hospitality groups,
developers, and public-realm teams buy a source-linked City Memory concept for
one real place or programme. A design or delivery partner may be the first
recipient even when a hotel, workplace, or public site is the visual subject.

## Four trust zones

Every activation moves through four explicit zones:

1. **Client collection** — bounded source material, scoped read access, and
   collection constraints; the source is not silently mutated.
2. **Private processing** — enrichment, retrieval, relationships, and
   hypotheses remain private and machine suggestions are not approved by
   default.
3. **Human review** — named curators or reviewers approve, edit, reject, or
   escalate suggestions, claims, rights, and release decisions.
4. **Client/public output** — only approved, rights-aware material crosses the
   release boundary with source, credit, license, uncertainty, and takedown
   information where applicable.

Every transition must preserve access, transformation, review, approval, and
release state. “No external model training” or local/private deployment may be
offered only when the actual delivery architecture can support the promise.

## Service ladder

The initial delivery model is a service ladder backed by reusable internal
software. Prices are indicative planning ranges, not authorization to quote.

| Offer | Path | Duration | Indicative range | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collection Fit Review | Institution | 2–3 weeks | $5,000–$12,000 CAD | Fit report, bounded enriched sample, recommended pilot scope |
| Archive Activation Pilot | Institution | 8–12 weeks | $35,000–$85,000 CAD | Reviewed cleared subset, preview, export package, methodology |
| City Memory Concept Study | Place | 4–6 weeks | $20,000–$60,000 CAD | Source-linked concept, visual territories, applications, rights and production path |
| Institution Plan | Earned later | Recurring | Do not quote yet | Ongoing enrichment, review, monitoring, and delivery after a completed pilot proves the need |

An optional $3,500 CAD, three-week diagnostic may be approved when the brief or
relationship is not mature enough for one of these offers. It must have
materially reduced scope, state its conversion path, and be credited toward a
larger engagement on agreed terms. It is not the default Provenance offer and
must not redefine a City Memory Concept Study.

Depending on the collection, an engagement may include:

- collection and metadata assessment;
- canonical record/family map;
- source, rights, claim, uncertainty, and transformation model;
- verified enrichment sample;
- one or more activation concepts;
- portable Provenance Package;
- implementation, licensing, and production roadmap.

Repeated service delivery should determine whether any component deserves to
become hosted software, an API, or workflow automation. No recurring plan,
SaaS surface, or agent platform is a V1 dependency; each must be earned from a
repeated, measured delivery need.

## Active roadmap

The active roadmap contains only:

- Provenance Activation product definition;
- Dataset Factory Core;
- Provenance Package;
- one client-sendable City Memory pilot using Hôtel Nelligan as the visual
  subject and reference context, without presuming it is the first recipient;
- buyer validation after the pilot passes internal release review.

Benchmarking, model promotion, Gate H execution, advanced feedback learning,
GPU work, and workflow automation are deferred research or operations. Their
historical artifacts remain useful but do not block the first client pilot.

## Product principles

- Lead with outcomes; keep technical machinery backstage.
- Preserve evidence, metadata, hypothesis, human review, and unknowns as
  distinct states.
- Make rights and attribution useful without claiming blanket legal clearance.
- Prefer bounded service delivery before platform development.
- Keep the institution and place paths commercially distinct while preserving
  one evidence and trust contract underneath.
- Prefer relationship-led introductions and a real buyer decision over cold
  platform outreach.
- Keep research benchmarks separate from client-release gates.
- Keep MTL Archives as the proof, not the only collection Provenance can serve.
- Reconsider workflow automation only after repeated delivery exposes a
  measured bottleneck.
